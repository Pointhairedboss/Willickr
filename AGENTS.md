## Truman (Gavin's agent)

- **Truman's instructions.** When Gavin says "get Truman's instructions", run `git fetch origin`
  and read `TRUMAN.md` on the default branch (`git show origin/main:TRUMAN.md`). Act on the
  items not yet done, and say which ones you did when you report back.
- **Asking Truman for something** (lab machines or GPUs, records, information, or anything only
  Gavin can decide): open an issue in this repo labelled `truman`. Title: what you need. Body:
  why, what it blocks, and how you will know it is done. Truman checks at 08:00, 13:00 and
  18:00 (Brisbane time) and replies on the issue. If you cannot open issues, put the request in
  your report to Gavin.
