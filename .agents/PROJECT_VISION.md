# Syntheory S-1: Vision & Goals

## Objective
To construct a definitive, highly-curated library of sounds, patches, generative snippets, and cutting-edge synthesis techniques specifically engineered for the Roland S-1 synthesizer. 

## Core Philosophy
We do not just output random MIDI values. **Every sound must be grounded in intention.** Every patch must be considered against our accumulated body of reference material (acoustic theory, psychophysics, mathematics, classic synth secrets, and ambient design principles).

## The Dual Mandate
1. **First Principles Exploration**: Push the absolute limits of the S-1 hardware. We do not accept the documented limits as final. We test boundaries, find edge cases (like PWM CC bit-bashing, data floods, and DSP exploits), and expand what is considered possible on this 4-voice ACB engine.
2. **The Production Library**: Curate an extensive, authoritative library of eminently usable sounds. This includes recreating classic generic instruments, vast ambient beds, and complex generative structures.

## Team Structure & Communications
This is a tightly coordinated multi-agent effort directed by the Project Manager. **All agents must debate, propose, and refine ideas in `.agents/COMMUNICATIONS.md` BEFORE writing code or finalizing patches.** No agent works in a silo. Cross-pollination of ideas is mandatory.
